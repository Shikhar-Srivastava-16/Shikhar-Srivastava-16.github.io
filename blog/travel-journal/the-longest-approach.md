title: The longest approach
date: 2024-11-02
summary: Three hours of switchbacks before sunrise, for about four minutes of usable light.
tags: himalaya, photography
---

The approach started at 4am, headlamp only, mostly to be in position
before the light did anything interesting. It's the kind of hike where
you stop noticing the cold about halfway up because you're too busy
counting switchbacks.

The light lasted four minutes. It was enough.
